Use the user's approved logo shown in these canonical project references:

- A dark chocolate-brown textured rectangular field.
- A thin double-line Art Deco border with stepped corner ornaments.

## Non-negotiable rule

Do not regenerate, redraw, reinterpret, recolor, filter, crop, stretch, re-typeset, or replace the logo during any other feature, layout, styling, caching, deployment, or refactor task. Do not substitute a merely similar record-player graphic.

Only change a logo or icon asset when the user explicitly requests a logo change in that task. Before any approved logo change, compare the result directly against the canonical reference above. Preserve its aspect ratio and complete composition wherever it is displayed.

During unrelated work, verify that branding files and `.brand-logo` presentation rules were not changed before publishing.
